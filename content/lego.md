## Cột chữ

***What keeps me building?***

My LEGO corner is where I enjoy a different kind of problem-solving. This F1 model brings together the details I like: the bodywork, wheels, wings, and small connections behind the finished build.

One piece at a time.

Building gives me practice slowing down, following a sequence, and paying attention to how each part fits. It reminds me that a complicated task becomes more manageable when I work through it step by step.

**Patience · Attention to detail · Step-by-step thinking**
