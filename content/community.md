## ARIA PROJECT / A CONCERT WITH A PURPOSE.

***HEAD OF FINANCE  |  APRIL–SEPTEMBER 2025***

***Nearly 200 attendees  ·  VND 60 million raised by the team***

I co-organized “Thanh Sac Viet — A Symphony of Five Continents,” a free concert at Hanoi College of Art. Our team donated the funds raised to the Cerebral Palsy Family Association Vietnam’s “Superhero Family Shelter,” supporting accommodation for families during their children’s treatment in Hanoi.

**Beyond the fundraising total, I gained experience connecting event planning with a specific community need. Working alongside the team strengthened my coordination and financial responsibility.**

## LOTUSMED 2025

***FINANCE TEAM  |  JANUARY–AUGUST 2025***

***Nearly 200 attendees  ·  VND 60 million raised by the team***

With TTH Vinh General Hospital and the LotusMed team, I helped coordinate health screenings for 50 girls from SOS Children’s Village Vinh. I monitored the fundraising budget as our team raised approximately US$2,000 for food and nutritional support.

**The practical lesson:** Tracking resources against program needs gave me experience with budget oversight. Coordinating with the hospital and project team strengthened my planning and communication.
