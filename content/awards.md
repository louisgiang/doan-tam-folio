## GOLD MEDAL  /  ICAN 2025

***Following the story in the data.***
*International Invention Innovation Competition in Canada* (gạch chân)

***The Relationship Between Past and Current Inflation in Vietnam: A View from Quantitative Analysis***

*How is current inflation in Vietnam related to its past?*

*As part of a student team supervised by Dr. Nguyen Thi Van Anh, I co-authored a quantitative study using CPI data from 2012–2024. My contribution included literature review, data processing, and helping develop recommendations for monetary–fiscal coordination.*

*From analysis to explanation: Working with the data strengthened my analytical skills. Presenting the research gave me practice explaining findings clearly and connecting an economic question with practical recommendations.*

## GOLD AWARD  /  ICPC 2026

***A wider view of economic growth.***
*International Creative Papers Conference & Olympic* (gạch chân)

***Green Economy and Vietnam’s Economic Growth***

*Our team examined economic growth and environmental pressures through Vietnam’s real GDP and carbon-emissions data from 1995–2024. I co-authored the study and took part in presenting it through a research poster and presentation.*

*The study’s approach: An Environmental Kuznets Curve framework and a quadratic model estimated in EViews.*

*Preparing the presentation gave me practice selecting the question, method, and findings an audience needs most. The project also connected my interest in economic growth with environmental questions.*

## ADDITIONAL STUDIES

**Cột trái**

**CO-AUTHOR  |  MAR–SEP 2025**
**Project: <u>Factors influencing consumers’ purchase decisions for official fashion: A case study in Hanoi</u>**
**Supervisors: Assoc. Prof. Hoang Thanh Tung and Dr. Nguyen Thi Van Anh**
**What shapes consumers’ fashion-purchasing decisions in Hanoi?**

I co-designed and analyzed a survey of 275 consumers using SmartPLS (PLS-SEM), assessing the survey measures and the relationships among purchase drivers. The study identified perceived behavioral control and subjective norms as its strongest predictors.

**Cột phải**

**CO-AUTHOR  |  DEC 2024 - AUG 2025**
**Project: <u>The impact of environment, social and governance (ESG) factors on firm financial performance of companies ranked  in the corporate sustainbility index (CSI)</u>**
**Supervisor: Dr. Dinh The Hung, National Economics University**

I co-authored research examining ESG factors and financial performance among companies ranked in the Corporate Sustainability Index. The topic brought my interest in finance into a study of corporate sustainability. Exploring this subject encouraged me to consider sustainability alongside financial performance. It broadened the questions I bring to studying companies without treating either perspective in isolation.
